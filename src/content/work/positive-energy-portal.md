Step into the Portal of Positive Energy and your presence wakes the ancient
oak in the Cambridge Sculpture Garden. Movement — any movement — nourishes
the tree, prompting it to answer in radiant pulses of light and sound.

The piece was built for Unsilent Night, and it borrows that event's central
idea: a work of art that only exists because people showed up and moved
through it together. There is no correct way to activate it. A child
spinning, someone rolling through in a wheelchair, two friends hugging in
the cold — the sensors read all of it the same way, and the oak lights up
either way.
