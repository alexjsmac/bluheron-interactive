Navitas — Latin for *energy* — is an interactive pop-up sculpture that moves
participants through the physical process of delivering drinking water to homes
across the City of Hamilton. Themes of clean, safe, and healthy drinking water
production and consumption are explored visually: treatment, testing, and
distribution within an urban environment.

The sculpture is constructed primarily from reclaimed and repurposed materials —
aluminum, copper, and wood — and lit by 2,000 LEDs, one for each kilometre of
the city's pressurized water system. It takes real, cooperative work to
operate: a teeter-totter hand pump that needs two people, and a hand-cranked
generator that keeps the power — and the water — flowing. The infrastructure we
never see becomes something a community operates together.
