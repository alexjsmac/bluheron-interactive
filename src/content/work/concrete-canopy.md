For Nuit Blanche London 2025, Concrete Canopy transformed the historic
Kingsmill's Department Store façade into a living digital organism.
Projection mapping followed the building's own geometry — columns, cornices,
window bays — so the growth appeared to come out of the stone rather than
sit on top of it.

The audiovisual elements were shaped live through a web application, letting
the piece change across the night as crowds moved through downtown London.
