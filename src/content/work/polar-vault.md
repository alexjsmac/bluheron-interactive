The Polar Vault transforms a standard shipping container into a glowing,
interactive winter wonderland in the middle of a downtown market square.
Touch-responsive walls, real-time visuals built with Three.js, and a
"shake to snow" mechanic driven by the visitor's own phone turn a photobooth
into something closer to a small weather system you can stand inside.

The queue runs itself. Visitors join from their phone, watch their place in
line, and step in when it's their turn — which meant a busy market night
never turned into a bottleneck, and no staff member spent the evening
managing a lineup.
