Murmuration is a live visual installation with no author on the night. The
projected scene is generated in real time out of whoever happens to be in the
room: each person joins from their own phone, and every gesture they make enters
a shared field where it mixes with everyone else's.

It was presented at Avant Mutek, at Mooi Space in Toronto, on May 30, 2026.
Joining takes seconds — a QR code on the wall, a web page, nothing to install —
which matters more than it sounds. An installation that asks for a download gets
the audience that already downloads things. One that runs in a browser gets the
whole room.

The name is the mechanic. A starling murmuration has no leader; the shape comes
out of thousands of birds each reacting to the handful nearest them. This works
the same way. You can find your own contribution in the projection for a second
or two, and then you lose it — not because it was discarded, but because it has
become part of something none of you could have aimed at. Nobody in the room is
directing the piece, and everybody is.
