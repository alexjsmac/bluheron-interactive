Most people never see a landfill up close, and the picture in their heads is
usually a hole full of garbage. The Glanbrook model replaces that with the
real thing: a working site with an active tipping face, daily cover, capped
and re-vegetated slopes, a compost facility, and a gas collection system
that turns decomposition into energy.

Built at model scale with touch points throughout, it lets a visitor lean in
and ask questions of a process that is otherwise fenced off and out of town.
The detail is the argument — when the machinery, the layers, and the
regrowth are all visible at once, waste management stops being an abstraction
and becomes a piece of civic infrastructure people can reason about.
