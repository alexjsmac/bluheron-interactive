A/V Nights is an immersive art showcase we founded and produce in London,
Ontario — live audiovisual performance alongside installations, experimental
film, and artist talks, staged in rooms that were never meant to be venues.

Night 1 landed at XUUX Artists Studio on September 13, 2025, co-presented with
Nuit Blanche London. Seven artists ran across three streams in one room: live
A/V sets from Sunntack, Drummachinemike with Anthony Gomez, and Matt Thibideau
with Alejandro Franco Briones; installations by the Frames Film Series and
Edward Platero; and presentations from Elburz Sorkhabi of The Immersive &
Interactive HQ and the London Society of Architects. Sixty tickets sold and
about eighty people came.

Night 2 moved to Honey Dip and DoughEV on May 2, 2026, and was staged as a
fundraiser for Forest City Gallery with lighting and production support from
Other World Productions. Mothers Against Drum Machines, Turbo Tambourine, and
Cailen and the Electric Bride played to live audio-reactive visuals by Toronto's
fongkikid. Downstairs, a programme of experimental film curated by Lianne
Zannier screened between sets while Gary Kirkham's "Polyrhythmic Illumination"
ran all night. The evening opened with an artist Q&A. Eighty tickets sold, about
a hundred people came, and the proceeds went to the gallery.

The reason to do this is the room, not the line-up. London has audiovisual
artists, electronic musicians, and immersive-media people who mostly work alone,
and it has audiences who are curious about that work but have no vocabulary for
it. Putting both in the same space — with the talks and the Q&A there so nobody
has to already be an insider — is the entire idea. The open call for Night 2
brought in more submissions than there were slots, which is the clearest
evidence we have that the room was needed.

Across two nights the series paid fourteen artists, drew roughly 180 people, and
worked with seven partner organizations. CBC covered Night 2 with a
[television segment](https://www.cbc.ca/player/play/video/9.7183335) and a
[London Morning radio feature](https://www.cbc.ca/listen/live-radio/1-158-london-morning/clip/16212112-londons-honey-dip-hosts-immersive-audiovisual-experience).
A/V Nights was made possible by the London Arts Council's Community Arts
Investment Program and the City of London, with additional support from the
Cambia Development Foundation.
