A/V Nights is an immersive art showcase bringing together live audiovisual
performances, art installations, and presentations from leading creators —
curated and produced by BluHeron with support from the London Arts Council.

We build these evenings for two audiences at once: the artists working in
immersive media around the region, who rarely get a room and a rig built for
their work, and the people who have never seen it and don't yet have the
vocabulary for it. The presentations are there so the second group leaves
knowing how the first group works.
