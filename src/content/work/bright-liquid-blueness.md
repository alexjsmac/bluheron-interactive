The Machine of Awesome Bright Liquid Blueness is an interactive water bottle
filling station built for the Art Gallery of Hamilton, exploring our
connection to the Great Lakes through play.

It works because it is genuinely useful. Nobody has to decide to engage with
it — they were going to fill their bottle anyway — and the game arrives in
the seconds they are already standing still. The lakes stop being scenery on
a map and become the thing currently coming out of the tap.
